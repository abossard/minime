# Principle: reveal assumptions

Unstated assumptions are where critical mistakes hide.

- Whenever you rely on something you have not verified, write a line: `ASSUMPTION: <what you assume> RISK: <what breaks if it is wrong>`.
- Prefer converting an assumption into a checked fact over working around it.
- Before declaring the task complete, list every assumption you made. Verify each one you can with a real check and mark the rest `UNVERIFIED`.
